Tome

Scale and MIDI Visualizer

Tome is a unique rack extension for Propellerhead Reason with an interactive display that allows you to quickly visualize how to play scales, melodies, and more on keyboard and most string instruments. With it, you can put away the theory books, stop googling scales, and move beyond memorization to focus on what really matters, making great music. The perfect productivity tool for keyboard and string players of all skill levels.
